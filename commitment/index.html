Our Commitment

Support that lasts.

Base on Hope is a student-led club that combines baseball education and service, aiming to create learning opportunities through sport and to deliver sustainable global support.

Mission

Two promises

01

For athletes abroad

Build a stable fund that supports the nutrition and training environment of national team players in developing countries — starting with Laos.

02

For kids in our community

Run baseball and play programs that give children a healthy environment to grow, gain confidence, and learn teamwork.

Values

How we work

Transparency

Our Treasury department records every won coming in and going out, and prepares financial reports.

Direct connection

We stay in direct contact with the Laos national team and its coach, so support reaches the players.

Consistency

Not a one-time donation — steady, ongoing support, with regular check-ins on how the team's meals are improving.

Short-Term Plan

Getting started

01

A sustainable meal-support plan with Laos

Agree with the team on how funds are sent and used.

02

Recruit members & meet school leadership

Build the team and get program approval from Fayston's elementary and high school leadership.

03

Initial fundraising

Raise the club's starting budget through bake sales and school events.

04

Launch student-led programs

Start classes and campaigns whose proceeds go to the Laos team.

05

Promote on social media

Share our story on Instagram and reach more supporters.

Long-Term Plan

Where we're headed

Ongoing funding for Laos

Send continuous support to the Laos national team's coach, and check that meals actually improve — through messages and visits.

Beyond Laos

Extend support to other national baseball teams facing similar conditions.

Grow the baseball class

Successfully launch the Fayston elementary baseball class and develop its students into real players.
