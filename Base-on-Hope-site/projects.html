BOH Play Class

A play-centered program that keeps elementary students moving during the break. Through foot baseball, dodgeball, jump rope, and relay games, kids learn teamwork, consideration, and fair play. All tuition goes toward meals for the Laos National Baseball Team.

Leads
Gyumin Jeong, Suhyun Park
Where
Fayston High School
Schedule
Once a week, 2 hours · 8 weeks
Who
All elementary grades
  • Weeks 1–2: icebreakers, target & focus games
  • Weeks 3–4: foot baseball, dodgeball, jump rope
  • Weeks 5–6: strategy tag games, relays
  • Weeks 7–8: challenge stations & BOH Play Olympics

BOH Summer Explorer Class

High school mentors plan and teach creative-thinking math, basic English grammar, and reading & book reports for elementary students, split into lower- and upper-grade classes. All tuition supports meals for the Laos National Baseball Team, so students learn that their learning can help others.

Leads
Minseo Park, Siyun Jeong
Subjects
Creative math (1h) · English grammar (1h) · Reading or homework help (30 min)
Math topics
Nemo-logic, pentomino, spatial puzzles, codes & ciphers, block puzzles, sudoku & kakuro, tangram, pattern finding

BOH Elementary Baseball Class

A hands-on elementary baseball team. Every session repeats the same structure — catch, batting, fielding, base running, and a real game — so kids naturally build the fundamentals. Communication, encouragement, manners, and sportsmanship are part of every step.

When
Every Saturday, 11 AM (2 hours)
Who
Elementary students
Coaches
Head coach Kim Dongmin + student coaches Suhyun Park, Seungwon Oh, Eden Je
  • Attendance & safety briefing (10 min)
  • Warm-up & stretching (10 min)
  • Fundamentals: fielding, catch, tee-batting, soft toss (40 min)
  • Real game practice — coaches pitch for safety (1 hour)
  • Cool-down & feedback (5 min)
